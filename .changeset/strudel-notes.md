`renderStrudel` says more about each note it played: its `duration`, its `gain`, and its `voice`, the name a score
gives a voice with `.voice("kick")`. A picture can now be drawn from the notes of one part, each on the moment it
sounds. The Strudel page built into the cache is named by its content, so a new version of it is built instead of an
older build being reused.
