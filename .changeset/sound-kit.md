The sound kit: `uberscene sound` lists cues (tick, click, pop, snap, whoosh, swish, suck, riser, downer, thud, hit,
boom, chime, shimmer) and beds (wind, rain, crackle, water, rumble, air) made in code, the same bytes on every
machine, and `uberscene sound <name> [--duration <s>] [--seed <n>]` writes one into the project and adds it.
`makeSound` and `soundWav` do the same from a script.
