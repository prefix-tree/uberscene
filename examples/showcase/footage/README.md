# Stock footage

The clips every moving picture in the showcase is cut from, committed as they are.
`../media.ts` checks each against its sha256 and trims it into `../media/`.

| File | Pixabay video | What it shows |
|---|---|---|
| `coffee.mp4` | [209419](https://pixabay.com/videos/id-209419/) | Pour-over coffee, close |
| `coffee-wide.mp4` | [209424](https://pixabay.com/videos/id-209424/) | Pour-over coffee, wide |
| `espresso.mp4` | [46989](https://pixabay.com/videos/id-46989/) | An espresso machine filling three glasses |
| `red-cup.mp4` | [251](https://pixabay.com/videos/id-251/) | Espresso into a red paper cup, with steam |

All four are under the [Pixabay Content License](https://pixabay.com/service/license-summary/), which allows use with
no credit and no fee. They are here to render this repository's examples; the licence does not allow taking a clip
from here and distributing it on its own. The maker of the machine in the red cup shot has nothing to do with this
project.
