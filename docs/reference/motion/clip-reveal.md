**What it conveys:** Arrival: here it is, revealed in one sweep.

## Use it when

- Bringing in a shot or a product image.
- A cleaner entrance than a fade.

## Values

| | Value | |
|---|---|---|
| crop.width | `0 to 1 over 1 s` | keys, easeInOutQuart: opens from the left |

## Ask your agent

> Reveal the shot from the left with keys on its crop.width from 0 to 1.
